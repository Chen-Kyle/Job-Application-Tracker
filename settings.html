Email updates

Choose how Gmail suggestions update your applications and how often to check for new emails.

i Info

Automatic mode approves new emails only when they clearly match one application and suggest a supported status change. Ambiguous matches, emails with next steps, and emails that only record activity still need your review.

Updates appear in Automatically added emails and the application’s email activity. Undo is available while newer changes have not made it unsafe. Gmail checks never send emails or change your mailbox.

Checks run while Chrome is open and Gmail is connected. Existing pending suggestions stay for manual review.

Backup and restore

Save your applications, dates, activity, and settings to a file, or restore a previous backup.

i Info

Backups include applications, dates, notes, next steps, status history, saved email subjects and links, recruiter contacts and conversation summaries, color scheme, and email preferences. They do not include Gmail authorization, pending or dismissed suggestions, scan progress, or undo history.

Restore adds missing applications. Existing applications keep their current information; matching IDs or job links are skipped. Select Restore backup’s settings to also apply its color scheme and email preferences.
